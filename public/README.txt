Drop your files here:

  profile.jpg   Your photo for the hero. Square works best (600x600 or
                larger). If this file is missing, the hero shows your
                initials instead — nothing breaks.

  resume.pdf    Linked from the "Resume" button in the hero and footer.

To use a hosted image instead of profile.jpg, set NEXT_PUBLIC_AVATAR_URL
in .env.local and add its hostname to `images.remotePatterns` in
next.config.ts.
